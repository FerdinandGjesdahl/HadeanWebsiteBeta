Oncoinvent (June 10, 2020)

Oncoinvent is a drug development company based in Oslo, Norway.  The company develops novel alpha‑emitting radioactive cancer treatments.  Oncoinvent’s lead product, Radspherin, is undergoing clinical testing for the treatment of peritoneal carcinomatosis, a common metastatic complication of colorectal and ovarian cancer【184411251660948†L26-L30】.
