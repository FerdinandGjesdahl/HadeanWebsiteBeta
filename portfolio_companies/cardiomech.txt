CardioMech (August 13, 2020)

CardioMech AS is a privately held Norwegian medical technology company located in Trondheim.  The company has developed a therapeutic solution for repairing mitral valve regurgitation.  CardioMech’s repair solution is aimed at repairing the chords of the mitral valve and is performed minimally invasively using a transcatheter and transatrial approach.
