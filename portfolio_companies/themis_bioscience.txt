Themis Bioscience (June 10, 2020)

Themis Bioscience is a vaccine development company based in Vienna, Austria.  Its lead product is a vaccine against the tropical disease Chikungunya, which is in late‑stage clinical testing.  The company also leveraged its vaccine platform technology to start developing a vaccine against Covid‑19.  Themis Bioscience was acquired by MSD in May 2020【761473497650763†L26-L32】.
