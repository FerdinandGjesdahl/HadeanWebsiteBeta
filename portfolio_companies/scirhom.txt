SciRhom (August 5, 2024)

SciRhom GmbH is a German biotechnology company focused on the research and development of novel therapeutics for immunology and inflammation (I&I).  The company’s lead product is an iRhom2‑targeting antibody, which offers a first‑in‑class opportunity for multi‑pathway inhibition while promoting immune and tissue homeostasis.
