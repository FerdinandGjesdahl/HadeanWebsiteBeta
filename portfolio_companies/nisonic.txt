Nisonic (June 10, 2020)

Nisonic is a medical device company from Trondheim, Norway developing a non‑invasive method for assessing increased intracranial pressure.  Using ultrasound and machine learning, the company detects whether patients have elevated pressure in the brain and aims to offer clinicians a measurement tool that could replace invasive sensors【937274946630186†L26-L30】.
