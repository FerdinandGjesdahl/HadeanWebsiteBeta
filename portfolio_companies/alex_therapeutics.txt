Alex Therapeutics (April 6, 2022)

Alex Therapeutics is a digital therapeutics (DTx) company based in Stockholm, Sweden.  It designs and develops Software‑as‑a‑Medical‑Device products.  With its “Alex DTx Platform”, expertise in artificial intelligence and evidence‑based psychology, the company is positioned to deliver cutting‑edge and reliable DTx products.  Alex Therapeutics has strategic commercial partnerships with global pharmaceutical companies, including Pfizer.
