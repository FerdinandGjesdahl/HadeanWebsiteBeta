ARTHEx Biotech (May 11, 2023)

ARTHEx Biotech is a Spanish biopharmaceutical company developing advanced RNA treatments against genetic diseases.  Its therapeutic approach uses an antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) to inhibit micro RNA‑23b, a key regulator for disease pathogenesis.
